We are the Intelligent System (IntelliSys) Lab at the ECE Department of Stevens Institute of Technology. Our research focuses on bridging artificial intelligence (AI) and emerging system architectures by developing efficient, robust algorithms and advancing intelligent, high-performance computing systems. Supported by multiple NSF grants, as well as funding and credits from AWS, NIH AIM-AHEAD, and the Computing Research Association (CRA), our work spans theory, algorithms, and real-world systems. Explore our projects.
